What to share and expect

An emergency plumbing call starts with the details you provide. A Littleton Emergency Plumber needs a useful description of the symptom, not a diagnosis from you. Depending on what you're seeing, the discussion may cover a leak, a fixture that will not drain, a toilet problem, or another plumbing concern. The specific work needed depends on the situation.

Have these details ready if you can:

  • The address where the problem is happening, including apartment or unit information
  • Which room or fixture is affected
  • When you first noticed the issue and whether it has changed

  • Any steps you've already taken

Ask what you should do while waiting for guidance. Avoid pouring chemical drain products into a fixture or repeatedly testing one that is already causing trouble. Those steps may make it harder to assess the issue or expose you to a mess.
